=== LOTE 5 — CLINCH (DOIS ATORES) ===
Gerar ATACANTE e SEGURADO em folhas SEPARADAS, mesma contagem de quadros, mesma célula. Os dois são o Sorriso
(o jogo troca a roupa do rival). Distância entre os pivôs: 22 px. Desenhar primeiro uma prévia composta
dos dois para conferir mãos na nuca e contatos; depois separar. Acrescentar ao cabeçalho:
"Two-actor synchronized clinch. This sheet shows ONLY the <attacker|held fighter>. The partner is NOT drawn,
but his position is 22 pixels in front (attacker) / behind (held). Hands must reach the partner's neck line."

clinch_reach (6, só atacante): arms reach for the opponent's nape and retract (also used when the grab misses).
clinch_enter / held_enter (8): hands lock behind the nape (muay thai plum), pull the head down; held fighter loses guard, neck bends.
clinch_hold / held_hold (6, ciclo): attacker elbows together, forehead close; held fighter pushes chest/hips, feet search for base.
clinch_drag_fwd / held_drag_back (8, ciclo): attacker pushes walking forward; held fighter stumbles back, feet follow the travel.
clinch_drag_back / held_drag_fwd (8, ciclo): attacker pulls backwards; held fighter is dragged.
clinch_knee / held_knee_hit (10): knee rises to the stomach keeping the two-hand grip; held fighter folds on impact; frame 4 = contact.
clinch_punch / held_punch_hit (8): one hand keeps the grip, the other throws a short punch to the face; frames 2-3 = contact.
clinch_elbow / held_elbow_hit (10): short horizontal elbow, THE ELBOW ARRIVES FIRST, hand close to own face; held head turns hard; frame 4 = contact.
clinch_escape / held_escape (10): held fighter wedges a forearm on the chest, pushes and frees himself; attacker loses the grip and steps back.
clinch_release / held_release (8): attacker lets go with a push; both return to guard at distance.
clinch_throw / held_thrown (14): pulls the head, finishing blow and release; held fighter falls and ends in down_back frame 0.
