Prompt:

White school polo green collar/sleeve trim, thin yellow/green shoulder bands and green hem, blue jeans, off-white sneakers. Slim believable human anatomy, NOT rubber limbs. SINGLE CHARACTER ALONE in every frame, facing RIGHT in 3/4 side-view, no opponent. TRUE transparent background, no text, labels, numbers, grid, shadows, speedlines, logos or gore. Make EXACTLY 8 columns x 6 rows, each frame occupying identical-sized cells. Camera/scale/body dimensions constant all 48 frames, stationary root pelvis relative to cell; full body fits with margin. Rows are six coherent 8-frame animations read left to right. Row1: tight short clinch punch: left hand gripping imaginary opponent at upper chest height, right arm retracts then short powerful straight to opponent body, recoil and recover, feet planted. Row2: clinch horizontal elbow: left hand maintains grip, torso turns, right elbow sweeps chest high and recoils, steady bones and feet. Row3: clinch knockdown punch: left hand releases grip, right hand chambers, hips/shoulders turn, full overhand punch forward-down, heavy follow-through, recovery step, no pose teleport. Row4: parry into counter: left open hand intercepts incoming attack near cheek, pivots it outward, right fist chambers then straight punch, return guard, stable proportions. Row5: hand cleaning: stands relaxed looking at right hand, wipes palm and knuckles using side/front of his jeans 3 times then returns guard, empty hands. Row6: grounded low finisher: plants left foot, raises right knee then extends right foot down-forward in controlled stomp against imaginary floor target, compresses knee on contact, lifts and recovers. Clear pixel clusters, sober rich pixel shading, clean contours, physically consistent joints, animation arcs and silhouettes. Do not copy the brown hair from reference 2; hair must read jet black with very dark charcoal subtle shading only. No new costume or accessories.

O último desenho do joelho vinha cortado na borda e foi substituído pela pose inicial na recuperação; são 87 quadros distintos novos referenciados, em 88 entradas. Limites:
